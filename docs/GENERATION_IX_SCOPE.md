# Generation IX Scope — Pokémon Violet

This repository is one title-specific branch of the complete Generation IX research and decompilation project. Pokémon Violet is not treated as interchangeable with Pokémon Scarlet; every shared or differing implementation must be demonstrated from evidence.

## Content boundaries in scope

- Base Pokémon Violet application and every verified historical revision
- Violet-specific data and version exclusives
- The Hidden Treasure of Area Zero
  - Part 1: The Teal Mask
  - Part 2: The Indigo Disk
- Epilogue / additional story-event content
- Limited-time and recurring Tera Raid Battle data where recoverable or documentable
- Mystery Gift / official distributions and related client-side data
- Ranked Battle / official online-rule data where represented client-side
- Pokémon HOME compatibility and transfer-related client behavior
- Nintendo Switch 2 behavior introduced or altered by later software updates

## Exhaustive reconstruction workstreams

1. Executable modules, code, functions, symbols and call relationships
2. Filesystem, containers, archives, compression and serialization formats
3. Pokémon species, forms, types, stats, abilities, evolution and form rules
4. Moves, move parameters and special processing
5. Abilities and special processing
6. Items, key items, held items, materials, TMs, food/picnic ingredients and recipes
7. Terastallization, Tera Types, Tera Raid Battle rules and event-raid data
8. Maps, fields, interiors, caves, collision, navigation and placement
9. Wild encounters, fixed encounters, outbreaks and encounter-condition data
10. NPCs, trainers, parties, battle conditions and rewards
11. Story scripts, events, flags, quests, classes, relationship events and postgame changes
12. Shops, auctions, TM Machine, picnic, sandwich, Let's Go, auto battle and ride systems
13. Graphics, models, textures, materials, animation, effects and UI
14. Text, message resources, localization and version-specific wording
15. Music, sound effects, voices where applicable and audio resource metadata
16. Union Circle, trades, battles, raids and other communication systems
17. Mystery Gift, distributions, live-event hooks and online-delivered content
18. Pokémon HOME integration and compatibility tables represented by the title
19. DLC and update-to-update differences
20. Unused, removed, legacy or unreachable data
21. Bugs, glitches and patch-specific behavior
22. Rebuild, validation, automated testing and reproducibility tooling

## Violet ↔ Scarlet rule

Equivalent-revision inventories are compared path-by-path and hash-by-hash. Common hashes may be treated as shared evidence; differing paths, hashes, scripts, text, maps, assets or parameters remain independent records until their relationship is demonstrated.

## Completion rule

A workstream is complete only after the defined target range has been exhaustively checked. Representative examples are not completion evidence. Unknown entries remain explicitly unresolved rather than omitted.
